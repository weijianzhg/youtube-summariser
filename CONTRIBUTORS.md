# Contributors

Thank you to everyone who has improved YouTube Summariser through code, feedback,
and ideas.

## Ideas and feature proposals

- [JWPapi](https://github.com/JWPapi) proposed channel-wide summarization in
  [PR #19](https://github.com/weijianzhg/youtube-summariser/pull/19), including a
  limit for processing only the most recent videos. Although the pull request was
  not merged, the proposal helped inspire the later `channel` command.
