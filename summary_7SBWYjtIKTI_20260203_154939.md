**Private Equity Risks** [30:45-36:00]  
Institutions prefer private equity's "low volatility" (infrequent pricing) over public market truth. But opacity masks higher actual volatility—these are leveraged investments marked by PE firms themselves. ~60-65% of PE transactions now PE-to-PE sales (circular market). Secondary markets trade at discounts, revealing mispricing. Some pension funds pressured to assume 7% returns (vs. realistic 5%) to avoid benefit cuts or tax increases.

**Retail/Meme Stock Power** [40:00-41:55]
